MetroVenture 3D

Work jobs → buy lots → launch startups → hire citizens → grow city employment. Now with cars you can drive!

On foot

  • W A S D / arrows: move
  • Shift: sprint
  • Mouse drag / Q R: orbit camera · wheel: zoom
  • E / Space: interact
  • F: get in/out of your car
  • V: valet. Brings your last car to you
  • C: character creator

Driving

  • W/S: gas / brake & reverse
  • A/D: steer
  • Space: handbrake (drift!)
  • G: horn · E: complete waypoints
  • F5 save · F9 load · M mute
  1. Your free Rusty Hatchback is parked at the curb. Walk up and press F.
  2. Clock in at the Express Courier Depot, City Security HQ or Metro Cab Co. (glowing rings).
  3. Buy a FOR SALE lot to launch a startup. Each one unlocks a new occupation.
  4. Walk up to unemployed citizens (or use the startup menu) to hire them. Collect profit every payday.
  5. Visit Metro Motors to buy faster cars, vans and taxis.
