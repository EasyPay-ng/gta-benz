MetroVenture 3D

Work jobs → buy lots → launch startups → hire citizens → grow city employment. Now with cars you can drive!

On foot

  • W A S D / arrows: move
  • Shift: sprint
  • X / P: toggle 1st-Person / 3rd-Person camera
  • Mouse drag / Q R: look / orbit · wheel: zoom
  • E: interact · Space: jump
  • F: get in/out of your car
  • B / 1–8: emotes & dances
  • V: valet · T: garage & roadside assist
  • U: Metro Grand University (1,105 courses)
  • Y: 3D Architect — build & furnish walls, floors, stairs, roofs & furniture, plus a full Minecraft block palette (18 blocks on a 1 m grid — stack them like voxels)
  • E at a door: open / close it · at a sofa, bed, TV, desk, gym machine or shop counter: use it (with a full animation — drink, eat, play the piano, take a pool shot…)
  • E at a TV or the cinema screen: the screen switches on and plays a channel; you take a seat facing it
  • E at a glowing Metro Lift: ride to any floor or the Sky Deck
  • C: character creator

Driving & 3D Cockpit

  • W/S: gas / brake & reverse · A/D: steer
  • X / P: switch between Outside Chase & 3D Cockpit Interior
  • Mouse drag / Q R in cockpit: look around cabin & mirrors
  • Space: handbrake (drift & tyre skids!)
  • G: horn · E: complete waypoints
  • Wrecking cars or reckless hits raise Police Heat (★–★★★) — outrun cruisers to cool down!
  • F5 save · F9 load · M mute
